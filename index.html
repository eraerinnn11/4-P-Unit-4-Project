Terraria Bosses (my top three)

Queen Bee

About

  • Pre-Hardmode boss
  • Summoned by destroying a larva in a beehive biome
  • She attacks by charging at the player or hovering above them and spawning bees


Plantera

About

  • Post-Hardmode boss
  • Summoned by breaking plantera's bulb in the jungle biome
  • She has two phases.
    In her first phase she clings to solid blocks and shoots seeds at the player;
    In her second phase, those seeds become poisonous and she spawns tentacles to attack you.


Moon Lord

About

  • Post-Hardmode boss (final boss)
  • Summoned by defeating the four celestial pillars or by using a celestial sigil (recommended to summon in front of your house)
  • His hands and eyes attack differently
    his hands shoot projectiles at the player (and summons phantom eyes to throw at the player)
    and his middle eye occasionally shoots a Phantasmal Deathray at the player which is usually instant death
